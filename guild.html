🤔 “I want to know.”

Curiosity means more than finding things interesting. It is a loop: wonder, ask, investigate, build, discover and wonder again.

🎯 The guild exists to turn curiosity into demonstrated capability: learn, build, test, debug, understand. Any structure that doesn't serve that is decoration.

🌌 One Philosophy, Three Scales

The same idea works for a person, a project and the whole guild.

🧑 Individual

Learn → Build → Break → Understand → Grow

⚔️ Project

Problem → Explore → Prototype → Engineer → Deploy → Feedback

🏹 Guild

Curiosity → Expeditions → Shared experience → Capability → New territories

🌱 We aren't trying to know everything. We're trying to become capable of exploring more.

🧬 Collective Memory

People graduate and projects end. The guild must remember what it learned after the people who learned it are gone. That is why we keep repositories, records and write-ups.

🗺️ Shared Direction, Autonomous Execution

Members choose what they explore and how they solve problems. The guild shares one direction, so different paths widen the map instead of fragmenting it.

⚖️ The Equal Floor

Everyone stands on the same floor. Rank may earn influence: whose voice carries and who leads quests. Rank never earns privilege. An equal floor with a ladder bolted on.

🔄 Succession

Leadership is handed over, never seized by default. The Master Architect and every officer choose their successor. No seat is permanent, including the top one: the Master Architect can be removed by their immediate predecessor and by the officers acting together.

🔓 Open Door, Closed Treasury

  • 🚪 Any student of the college may apply. Everyone starts at the bottom, with no entrance exam.
  • 💰 The guild holds no money and charges no fees.
  • 🏫 The guild lives inside the college and doesn't pretend to stand above it.

🛠️ Roles

🧭 Master Architect
Looks after the guild's direction, standards and ambition, without micromanaging. Chooses a successor when the time comes. Alone issues the guild-wide quest, has the final voice when a member is accused of harming the guild, and interprets unclear rules.
🐛 Debug Officers
Keep work moving: coordination, collaboration, quests, debugging help and code review. They also judge promotions, testing whether a member's capability is real. One per branch per year as the guild grows.
🛡️ Git Staff
Look after the guild's technical memory: repositories, version control and the full write-up of projects for the next generation. They confirm that quests are actually complete. Two per year of study as the guild grows.

Every officer is also a member with a rank. The title adds responsibility, not status.

See them on the 👥 Members page.

🧠 How We Learn

🔍 Understand the mechanism

Knowing JavaScript is less useful than knowing where it sits: browser, network, operating system, hardware. We take systems apart (decomposition) and build them back up (construction).

🛠️ Tools are allowed

AI and third-party code are fine if credited and understood. The test: can you explain it? If not, you borrowed it rather than built it.

💥 Failure is data

  1. 🔮 Predict
  2. 🔨 Build
  3. 💥 Meet reality
  4. 🐛 Debug
  5. 🔎 Find the hidden assumption
  6. 🧠 Improve the model
  7. 🔁 Try again

Being wrong is fine; learning nothing from it isn't. We explain why we believe something and aim for better models, not just more information. Watch this on the 📜 Quest Board.

Honest bugs and honest failure are never misconduct. Dishonesty is: claiming another's work or hiding what you did.

🗣️ Socratic learning

  1. ❓ Question
  2. 🧠 Model
  3. 🗣️ Explain
  4. 🔎 Inspect
  5. ⚠️ Find the gap
  6. ❓ New question

🌱 Culture & Values

Kautūhala is in its seed phase 🌱 with up to 20 members. We're building our first project and learning how we work together; the seed phase ends when the guild's website goes live and the Master Architect declares it. Then the doors open to every eligible student.

We aim to avoid:

  • 🚫 Status games
  • 🚫 Technology worship
  • 🚫 Conformity
  • 🚫 Superficial participation
  • 🚫 Building without purpose

📉 Going Quiet

Activity is evidence. The newest, least-proven members are first to lose their place if they vanish, mid-ranks slip a step, and proven members and founders are not punished for silence. Those who leave or are removed wait out a cooling-off period and return at the start.

🚨 When Someone Harms the Guild

  • Harassment, discrimination, abuse of rank or power and passing off others' work as your own damage a guild.
  • Only officers bring a case. Any member can raise a concern with any officer, and the accused always replies before anything is decided.
  • The Master Architect's options are narrow: dismiss, drop a rank, or exile. Exile is rare by design.
