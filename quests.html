🎯 What Makes a Good Quest?

❌ Learn React → ✅ Build something that forces you to understand X.

A quest can belong to one member (solo), a small party (squad), or the whole guild (a guild-wide quest, issued by the Master Architect alone). Ask: would it still be worth building if nobody at college cared?

🧱 Principles of the Quest System

  1. The board is curated: only Debug Officers and the Master Architect post quests.
  2. Every quest has a rank, slightly above comfort: one rank up, never more.
  3. Parties are small, so every member is load-bearing. One scribe per party documents the work and talks to the Git Staff.
  4. A quest counts only if confirmed.
  5. Credit is sacred: people are credited for what they build.
  6. Bugs are expected. Hunting them is half the game.
